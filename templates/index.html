A software developer shaped by real operational experience.

I'm a software developer with 11 years of experience at Chick-fil-A, where I've grown through leadership, operations, training, and financial roles.

That eventually grew into a much deeper interest in software engineering itself. I'm self-taught, primarily work in Go and TypeScript, and enjoy understanding systems from the application layer down through databases, networking, Linux, and infrastructure.

I like building practical software. Most of the applications I've created started with a real problem I wanted to solve.
