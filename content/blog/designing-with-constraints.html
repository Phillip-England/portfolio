Constraints are quiet collaborators. They trim indecision, sharpen intent, and force clarity.

Less surface, more signal

When I design a page, I cap the palette, cap the font stack, and cap the number of sections. That small box is where the texture appears. The work becomes about hierarchy, rhythm, and spacing rather than decoration.

Small motion, big feel

Animations should never compete with content. Subtle movement on page load and a soft transition between pages is enough to make a static site feel alive.

  • Introduce only one accent color.
  • Let whitespace do the heavy lifting.
  • Make your navigation predictable, not loud.

Minimal does not mean empty. It means intentional.
